--- title: About Hockey Network USA description: Who we are, what we cover, and why. ---

Hockey Network USA is a site about hockey in Utah, written for people who love it, or are just starting to.

Some of us remember the Golden Eagles at the Salt Palace, the Grizzlies at the E Center, and that February in 2002 when the whole world watched hockey in West Valley City and Provo. Some of us are brand new, hooked by a Mammoth game or a kid who wants to play. Either way, there has never been a better time to be a hockey fan here.

We cover two things. The first is the Utah Mammoth: a report every Monday, plus a schedule and standings page that refreshes every morning. The second is everything at ice level: where to skate, how kids get started, which adult leagues take beginners, and how to learn to skate at any age. Every so often we take a trip down memory lane, too.

We are not affiliated with the NHL, the Utah Mammoth, USA Hockey or any rink. Team names are used only to identify who we are writing about.

Got a rink memory, a correction, or a league we should list? Email hello@hockeynetworkusa.com. We read every one.
