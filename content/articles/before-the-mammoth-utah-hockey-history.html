When the NHL arrived in 2024, a lot of people talked like hockey was brand new to Utah. If you were around in the '70s, '80s or '90s, you knew better. This state has been a hockey town for more than 50 years. It just took the rest of the world a while to notice.

The Golden Eagles and the Salt Palace

From 1969 to 1994, the Salt Lake Golden Eagles were Utah hockey. They played in the Western Hockey League, then the Central Hockey League, then the International Hockey League, and for most of that run they called the Salt Palace home. That round arena downtown was where a whole generation saw their first live hockey game.

They won, too. The Golden Eagles took Adams Cups as Central Hockey League champions in 1975, 1980 and 1981, then back-to-back IHL Turner Cups in 1987 and 1988. If you were in the building for any of those, we'd love to hear about it.

The team moved into the brand-new Delta Center in 1991. The Salt Palace arena came down in 1994, and that same year the Golden Eagles left town to become the Detroit Vipers. It felt like the end of something.

{{photo:grizzlies}}

The Grizzlies years

It wasn't the end for long. The Utah Grizzlies arrived in 1995 and kept pro hockey alive for three decades, first in the IHL, then the AHL from 2001 to 2005, then the ECHL from 2005. In 1997 a new arena opened in West Valley City, called the E Center back then and the Maverik Center today. For a lot of families, a Grizzlies game was the affordable night out: cheap seats, a hot dog, a promo giveaway, a kid on your lap banging the glass.

2002: the world comes to Utah

Then came the Salt Lake 2002 Winter Olympics. Olympic hockey was played at the E Center and at Peaks Ice Arena in Provo. Canada beat the host United States in both the men's and women's gold medal games. It hurt, but those were some of the most exciting hockey games ever played in this state. Peaks is still open for public skating today.

{{photo:e-center-2002}}

The Games left more than memories. The Utah Olympic Oval in Kearns is still open to the public. You can lace up rentals where Olympians raced.

{{photo:olympic-oval}}

And now

Then, in 2024, the Utah Hockey Club played its first NHL game at Delta Center, the same building where the Golden Eagles played their last seasons. The team became the Mammoth a year later. On New Year's Eve 2026 they play an outdoor Winter Classic at Rice-Eccles Stadium. In 2034, the Olympics come back.

From the Salt Palace to the Winter Classic, it's been a long road. Utah hockey didn't start in 2024. It grew up.
