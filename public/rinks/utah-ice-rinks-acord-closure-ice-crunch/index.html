Utah's ice time crunch: Acord closure and what it means for skaters

Acord Ice Center is closed for repairs and Salt Lake County changed its public skate rules. What happened, and where to skate in the meantime.

Updated November 1, 2026 · How we make money

Every rink has its own smell. Cold air, rubber mats, popcorn from the snack bar, a little Zamboni exhaust. For a lot of families in the west valley, that smell meant Acord Ice Center. Birthday parties, first lessons, Saturday morning rec games, a lot of hot chocolate from the vending machine. So it stung when the doors closed this summer.

What happened at Acord

Salt Lake County closed Acord Ice Center in West Valley City on Aug. 11, 2026, after what it called a catastrophic failure in the rink's cooling tower. The Salt Lake Tribune reported repairs are expected to take six to nine months, and that keeping the ice frozen on generators would have cost about $100,000 a month. The county says it is committed to reopening.

Acord was home to youth rec hockey for ages 6 to 14, adult leagues, and drop-in and stick-and-puck sessions. All of those skaters are now squeezing onto the county's other sheets.

A new rule at public skate

Since the start of September 2026, Salt Lake County doesn't allow private coaching during public skate sessions. The county says it is about capacity. Some coaches worry it pushes brand-new skaters onto busy ice with advanced ones, and it adds pressure to learn-to-skate classes that already have waitlists of several months.

It's a funny moment. Utah is getting ready to host the world again at the 2034 Winter Olympics, and right now it is hard for a kid to find open ice.

Where to skate for now

  • Salt Lake County: Salt Lake City Sports Complex in Sugar House, County Ice Center in Murray, the Utah Olympic Oval in Kearns and Cottonwood Heights Ice Arena.
  • North: South Davis Recreation Center in Bountiful, the Weber County Ice Sheet in Ogden and the George S. Eccles Ice Center in North Logan.
  • South and east: Peaks Ice Arena in Provo, which hosted Olympic hockey in 2002, and Park City Ice Arena.
  • Outdoors, in season: the Gallivan Center downtown, Millcreek Common and the Ice Ribbon in Herriman. Nothing beats a lap under the lights with the cold on your cheeks.

Our Utah rink directory lists them all, with what each one offers and a link for current hours.

Tips for finding ice

  • Weekday afternoon public sessions are usually quieter than evenings and weekends.
  • Sign up for lessons the day registration opens, and get on waitlists at more than one rink.
  • A drive to Provo, Ogden or Park City might be worth it. Make it a family outing.

Sources: Salt Lake Tribune (Sept. 8, 2026) and Salt Lake County Parks and Recreation. We'll update this page when Acord reopens.
