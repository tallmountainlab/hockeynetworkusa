How we make money

The site is free to read. We pay for it in three ways.

  • Affiliate links. Some links to tickets and hockey gear are partner links. If you buy through one, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. They are labeled "Partner link."
  • Local sponsors. Utah businesses can pay for a clearly labeled sponsor spot. Sponsors never decide what we write.
  • Ads. We may show display ads from Google.

We only point to products and sellers we would send a friend to. Questions? Email hello@hockeynetworkusa.com.
