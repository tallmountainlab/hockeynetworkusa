Youth hockey

Almost every hockey player you've ever cheered for started the same way: wobbly skates, a helmet two sizes too big and a parent holding a coffee by the glass. Here's where that starts around Boston.

  • NHL/NHLPA Learn to Play: beginner programs run with the NHL teams, gear often included. Find your team's program
  • USA Hockey: the national governing body's guide for new hockey families, with local associations. New to hockey
  • Your local rink: most rinks above run their own learn-to-play and in-house leagues.

Adult hockey

It is never too late. Plenty of people lace up for the first time in their 30s and 40s, and beer league hockey has a level for everyone.

  • Rinks: most rinks on this list run adult leagues, drop-in hockey and stick-and-puck sessions. Look for those words on their sites.
  • USA Hockey: adult players register here for insurance and sanctioned leagues. Players and parents

Learn to skate

Everyone starts on the boards. Learn to Skate USA classes run at rinks across the country, for kids and adults, and they're the fastest way to get comfortable on the ice.

  • Learn to Skate USA: get started
  • Public skate sessions at the rinks above are the cheapest way to practice between lessons.
