Youth hockey

In Canada the first step usually has a name: First Shift, the NHL/NHLPA program that gets kids head-to-toe gear and a few sessions on the ice. After that, it's your local minor hockey association.

  • NHL/NHLPA First Shift
  • Hockey Canada lists provincial branches and minor associations.
  • Most rinks above also run learn-to-play and house leagues.

Adult hockey

Rec hockey is a way of life up here, from 6 a.m. weekday skates to Sunday night beer league.

  • Rinks: most rinks on this list run adult leagues, shinny and drop-in hockey.
  • Outdoor rinks: when it's cold enough, the outdoor rinks on this list are where pickup shinny lives.

Learn to skate

Skate Canada's CanSkate program is how most Canadians learned, and adult classes are easy to find too.

  • Skate Canada lists clubs and CanSkate programs.
  • Public skating at the rinks above is the cheapest way to practice.
